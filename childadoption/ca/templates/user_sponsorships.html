About Lifetime Sponsorship
What is Lifetime Sponsorship?

Lifetime sponsorship is a long-term commitment to support a child's needs throughout their journey with our organization.

Benefits:
  • Continuous support for the child's basic needs
  • Education and healthcare support
  • Regular updates on the child's progress
  • Direct communication with the care organization
  • Ability to fulfill specific needs through our platform
Tip: You can view and fulfill specific needs for each sponsored child by clicking on their card.
